# Chapter 1. Overview

## 1.1. Rationale for Choosing the Topic

Nowadays, IoT (Internet of Things) technology is used in many fields, including business and management. Connecting devices to software helps users monitor their operation and manage related activities more easily. Recognizing the potential of this technology, our group decided to choose a topic related to perfume sampling and management using an IoT-based system.

When buying perfume, many customers want to try a fragrance first to see whether it suits them. However, managing perfume sampling requires stores to keep track of the amount of perfume remaining, the operating status of the machines, and the transactions that take place. When multiple machines or perfume brands use the same system, managing this information can become more complicated.

For these reasons, our group chose the topic ScentStation - A Multi-Brand IoT Perfume Experience and Management Platform. The project aims to develop a system that allows customers to select a fragrance, pay using a QR code, and try the perfume through an automated machine. In addition, the system supports the management of products, devices, transactions, remaining perfume quantities, and maintenance activities. This helps perfume brands monitor and manage their sampling machines more conveniently.

Through this project, our group also hopes to apply the knowledge gained from studying system analysis and design, programming, databases, and API development. The project provides an opportunity for team members to practice teamwork, coordinate the development of different features, and gain a better understanding of the software development process.
