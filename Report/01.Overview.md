# Chapter 1. Overview

## 1.1. Rationale for Choosing the Topic

<!-- Content. Retrieved from docs/nhat-ky/ -->
Nowadays, IoT (Internet of Things) technology is used in many fields, including business and management. Connecting devices to software helps users monitor their operation and manage related activities more easily. Recognizing the potential of this technology, our group decided to choose a topic related to perfume sampling and management using an IoT-based system.

When buying perfume, many customers want to try a fragrance first to see whether it suits them. However, managing perfume sampling requires stores to keep track of the amount of perfume remaining, the operating status of the machines, and the transactions that take place. When multiple machines or perfume brands use the same system, managing this information can become more complicated.

For these reasons, our group chose the topic ScentStation - A Multi-Brand IoT Perfume Experience and Management Platform. The project aims to develop a system that allows customers to select a fragrance, pay using a QR code, and try the perfume through an automated machine. In addition, the system supports the management of products, devices, transactions, remaining perfume quantities, and maintenance activities. This helps perfume brands monitor and manage their sampling machines more conveniently.

Through this project, our group also hopes to apply the knowledge gained from studying system analysis and design, programming, databases, and API development. The project provides an opportunity for team members to practice teamwork, coordinate the development of different features, and gain a better understanding of the software development process.

## 1.4. Target Users

ScentStation is designed for different groups of users, from customers who want to try perfumes to those responsible for managing and operating the system. Each group has its own responsibilities depending on its role and scope of management.

### 1. Customers / Kiosk Users

These are customers who want to try a perfume before deciding whether to purchase it. Through the sampling machine, customers can view perfume information, choose their favorite fragrance, pay using a QR code, and receive one spray after the payment is successful.

### 2. Platform Super Administrators

Platform Super Administrators are responsible for managing the entire ScentStation platform. They manage the participating perfume brands, monitor overall system activities, and configure necessary settings. They also ensure that each brand's data is managed separately.

### 3. Brand Administrators

Each perfume brand has administrators who manage its related activities. Their responsibilities include updating product information, adjusting prices, managing users, monitoring machine locations, and viewing reports about the brand's activities.

### 4. Operations Managers

Operations Managers are responsible for monitoring the operating status of perfume sampling machines and the transactions that take place. When a machine encounters a problem or a transaction issue occurs, they check the information, review alerts, and coordinate with relevant teams to resolve the issue.

### 5. Technicians

Technicians are responsible for inspecting and maintaining perfume sampling machines. When a problem occurs, they identify the cause, carry out repairs, record the results, and test the equipment before returning the machine to normal operation.

### 6. Inventory Staff

Inventory Staff are responsible for monitoring the amount of perfume used in the sampling machines. They record perfume refills and bottle replacements, check the remaining perfume quantity, and ensure that the correct perfume is installed in each machine slot.
